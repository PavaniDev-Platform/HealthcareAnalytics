# Emergency Department Analytics

## Project Overview

Healthcare Emergency Department Analytics is an end-to-end analytics solution built to evaluate operational efficiency, patient outcomes, departmental performance, and emergency care quality.

The project transforms raw emergency department encounter data into actionable business insights using a layered architecture consisting of SQL Views, a Data Access Layer (DAL), a Business Layer, and an interactive Dash dashboard.

The solution enables hospital administrators, operational managers, and quality teams to answer critical business questions related to emergency department utilization, patient satisfaction, staffing effectiveness, readmissions, insurance coverage, and quality of care.

## Executive Overview

The Executive Overview focuses on hospital wide performance indicators and financial outcomes.

### Key Questions Answered

- How many emergency admissions occur in each department?
- What is the average triage wait time by department?
- What are the satisfaction scores across departments?
- What are the 30-day readmission rates by department?
- How much revenue is generated by each department?

### Insurance Mix Analysis

The dashboard also provides insights into the impact of insurance coverage on emergency department performance.

- Admission trends by insurance type
- Average triage wait times by insurance type
- Patient satisfaction by insurance category
- 30-day readmission rates by insurance category
- Amount billed to insurance providers

## Operations Overview

The Operations Overview evaluates departmental workload, patient severity, staffing efficiency, and emergency department utilization.

### Key Questions Answered

- Which departments are the busiest?
- Which departments handle the highest number of critical emergencies?
- Which departments may be understaffed or overstaffed?
- How are physicians distributed across critical and non-critical cases?
- What is the average length of stay for each department?

### Critical vs Non-Critical Emergency Analysis

Patient severity is analyzed using Emergency Severity Index (ESI) classifications.

- Critical vs non-critical emergency volume by department
- Physician allocation across severity levels
- Length of stay comparison for critical and non-critical emergencies
- Satisfaction comparison for critical and non-critical patients
- Readmission comparison for critical and non-critical patients

## Emergency Severity Index (ESI)

The project incorporates Emergency Severity Index (ESI) classifications to distinguish critical and non-critical emergencies.

| ESI Level | Classification |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Most Critical, life-threatening condition |
| 2 | Emergent, high-risk condition |
| 3 | Urgent, requires multiple hospital resources |
| 4 | Less Urgent, requires limited resources |
| 5 | Non-Urgent, requires minimal resources |

For dashboard analysis:

- Critical Emergencies = ESI 1 and ESI 2
- Non-Critical Emergencies = ESI 3, 4, and 5
