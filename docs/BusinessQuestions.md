TRIAGE LEVEL:
    Most hospital use ESI(Emergency Service Index) which has value 1 to 5 
    1 being the most critical:
    2 Emergent-High-risk situation where the patient is stable for the moment, but the condition could quickly become life-threatening.
    3 Urgent-Stable but the patient needs multiple hospital resources (such as lab work, X-rays, or IV medications).
    4 Less Urgent-Stable and requires only one hospital resource.
    5 Non Urgent-Stable and require no hospital resource other than general physician examination and prescription.
